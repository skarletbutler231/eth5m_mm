    Working orders — paper

    Paper fills against the live book. Each order takes the latency to reach the book, is rejected if it would cross (post-only), and joins the back of its price level. It moves up as prints at its price eat the queue and when the level shrinks below it; cancels are assumed to come from behind it. It fills when prints at its price pass the queue (queue), when a taker prints through its price (through), or when the other side rests on its price (cross). Prints come from both tokens, Down mapped to Up terms (each match prints on one token only). A cancel in flight can still fill.

    Fair value scorecard — does FV predict the market?

    lead: gap g = FV − mid now, move d = mid h later, on seconds with a live ≤3¢ book. hit = share of |g| ≥ 1¢ where the mid moved toward FV (50% = no information); β = how much of the gap the mid closes. A mid that flickers and snaps back also scores here, so read it with settlement: Brier and log loss once a second against the real outcome (lower is better). FV below the market on settlement = FV knew something the book did not.
